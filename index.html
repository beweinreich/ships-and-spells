📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎮 CONTROLS

Move: left joystick (mobile) or WASD/arrows (desktop).
Aim & Attack: right joystick, or mouse + click.
Talk / Interact: the 💬 button, or E, when near an NPC.
Boost / Sprint: double-tap the left joystick, or hold Shift. 5s burst, 30s cooldown.

🧙 WIZARD vs 🏴‍☠️ PIRATE

Wizards cast ranged spells learned from sages, and can craft powerful gear at the Arcane Forge using 💎 gems and ⚙️ metal.
Pirates fight with melee & ranged weapons, collect 🪵 wood and 💰 gold, build ships, and construct upgrades at port towns.

🏡 SAFE ZONES

The starting village and every named town are safe by day — no monsters will attack you inside them. At night, safe zones fall — monsters can raid towns too, so stay alert or fight back!

🌙 DAY / NIGHT

Night makes monsters stronger, faster, and more aggressive — but you earn 2× XP. Watch the sky darken and be ready.

🗺️ EXPLORING

Six named towns plus dock waypoints are scattered across the world, each with its own biome, monsters, shops, and lore. Visit them to unlock new spells, weapons, and upgrades. Your minimap (top corner) shows nearby towns and terrain.

⚔️ COMBAT, CRITS & COMBOS

Every hit shows floating damage numbers. Roughly 1 in 7 hits is a CRIT — 1.8× damage, shown big and red.
Landing hits without pausing builds a COMBO counter at the top of the screen. It resets after 2.5s with no hits. Kill something at 5+ combo for bonus gold.

🎯 BOUNTIES

Visit the 📜 Bounty Board in Emberwick to accept a bounty — slay a set number of a specific monster for a gold and XP reward. Progress tracks automatically as you fight. Turn it in and grab another for a fresh goal every run.

🔥 NIGHT SIEGES

Some nights a horde marches on the nearest town. Siege monsters glow red and are tagged 🔥 SIEGE, and a pulsing ring marks the town under attack. Repel them all before dawn for a big reward — fail and the town gets raided.

💀 DEATH & LEGACY

Dying in a safe zone keeps your gold. Dying out in the wilds costs you half your gold and gems — so bank your loot in town when you're carrying a lot!
Each death earns ⭐ Legacy Points (more for higher levels). Spend them on permanent perks that carry across every future life: Vitality, Fortune, Swiftness, and Alchemy.

📖 BESTIARY

Open the DEX tab to see every monster in the world. Undiscovered ones show as ❓ — kill one to reveal its stats and fill in your collection. Can you find all of them?

⚔️ GEAR & APPEARANCE

Every kill has a chance to drop gear — a new sword or wand, an outfit, or an accessory. Rarer drops (Rare, Epic, Legendary) glow brighter and hit harder. Mega beasts always drop Epic+.
Open your Bag to equip what you find — your weapon and hat appear on your character, your outfit becomes a visible cape, and rare+ gear gives you a glowing aura. Outfits also boost max HP, and weapons/accessories boost damage and crit chance. Gear resets each life, so every run looks different.

🌐 CO-OP

Tap 🌐 CO-OP on the title screen. One player hosts and gets a 4-letter room code plus a share link — send either to your friend. They tap the link (or type the code) and you're connected.
Works on two phones anywhere, on WiFi or mobile data. The host simulates the world; if either of you dies you respawn in Emberwick and the run continues.

⛵ SHIPS

Pirates can gather wood to build a Sloop (and later a Brigantine) at the Shipwright, unlocking travel across the ocean.

🧩 OMNIBUS-7

A deep, dangerous dungeon for wizards only, guarded by riddles. Solve them to progress — flee and you'll take damage instead.
